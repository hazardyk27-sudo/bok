- 2026-09-29 — Ticket sale transaction rules accepted — Players may sell any quantity of tickets they currently own; sales are not restricted to preset percentage amounts. The authoritative sale price is the global server ticket price at the exact moment the backend receives the sell request. Settlement is atomic: remove the sold ticket quantity from inventory and credit the resulting cash to the shared wallet in one backend transaction; if either side fails, the entire sale is rolled back. If a 5-second market tick changes while a request is in flight, the server price at request receipt/processing is authoritative. Client-submitted prices are never trusted.
- 2026-09-29 — BTC market data source architecture accepted — The global ticket market uses server-side public BTC market data, never per-client polling. Primary source: Binance BTCUSDT public WebSocket market stream. Backup source: Coinbase BTC-USD public WebSocket. The backend keeps the latest BTC price in memory and samples it every 5 seconds for the 15x ticket-price transformation. Implement automatic reconnect/resubscribe, Binance 24h connection rotation handling, stale-feed detection, and provider failover. On provider switch, re-baseline to the new provider's current BTC price so exchange-to-exchange price differences do not create an artificial ticket-price jump; only subsequent percentage moves are applied. If both feeds are unavailable, freeze the last valid ticket price, do not synthesize movement, and when a feed recovers re-baseline before resuming. Clients never connect directly to the exchange and never submit authoritative BTC or ticket prices. This is a resilience design, not a guarantee that an external provider can never have an outage.
- 2026-09-29 — BTC-driven ticket market bounds accepted — The global ticket market samples BTC every 5 seconds and applies BTC's full 5-second percentage move multiplied by 15x, with no per-tick volatility cap or smoothing limit. Raw ticket price update: newPrice = oldPrice × (1 + 15 × btcReturn5s). The only hard bounds are $0.20 minimum and $10.00 maximum; after each raw update, clamp to [$0.20, $10.00]. Example: BTC -10% in a 5-second window => ticket raw move -150%; raw price can fall below zero and is then clamped directly to $0.20. BTC direction/magnitude is otherwise followed without limiting the rise/fall size.
- 2026-09-29 — Global ticket market cadence accepted — Ticket price is global/shared for all players and updates every 5 seconds. The market should behave like a crypto-style price series: small continuous tick-to-tick movements, persistent short/medium trends, volatility clustering, occasional larger moves, and rare spikes/crashes, but no implausible huge jump within a single 5-second tick. Market settlement remains server-authoritative.
