CRITICAL FREE SPIN CALCULATION BAR REDESIGN + ANIMATION PATCH

Modify the EXISTING CASCADE 8 project.

DO NOT create a new project.

DO NOT change RTP.

DO NOT change payout math.
DO NOT change Free Spin count logic.

This patch is ONLY about the FREE SPIN CALCULATION PRESENTATION and its ANIMATION / SOUND / TIMING behavior.

The current Free Spin calculation bar is not acceptable.

Problems:
- visually weak
- too heavy and boxy
- placed below the board instead of above it
- too static
- no readable motion
- no clear flow of “symbol win -> multiplier -> spin win”
- no satisfying accumulation feeling
- no strong audio feedback
- everything resolves too quickly and unclearly

We want a much more premium, readable, animated system inspired by the clarity and satisfaction of high-quality tumble slots.

==================================================
1. MOVE THE FREE SPIN CALCULATION BAR ABOVE THE BOARD
==================================================

The Free Spin calculation UI must be moved ABOVE the board, not below it.

It should sit closely above the 6x5 grid and below the top HUD.

The current position below the board is wrong.

Correct visual order during Free Spins:

TOP HUD
FREE SPIN CALCULATION BAR
MAIN BOARD
TOTAL BONUS WIN
BOTTOM CONTROLS

This makes the live current-spin calculation easier to notice while watching the board.

==================================================
2. CALCULATION BAR MUST BE COMPACT AND PROPORTIONAL
==================================================

The bar must not be too tall or too bulky.

It should be compact, elegant, and clearly readable.

Requirements:
- width visually proportional to the board
- aligned with the board width
- moderate height
- not a giant thick dashboard slab
- not visually heavier than the board itself

Target concept:
a slim premium information strip / card above the board.

==================================================
3. LAYOUT OF THE CALCULATION BAR
==================================================

The bar should clearly show:

SYMBOL WIN   ×   MULTIPLIER   =   SPIN WIN

This must remain visually obvious at all times during Free Spins.

Structure example:

[ SYMBOL WIN ]
      ×
[ MULTIPLIER ]
      =
[ SPIN WIN ]

Or a clean horizontal row version:

SYMBOL WIN      MULTIPLIER      SPIN WIN
  20.00     ×      15x      =      300.00

Choose the version that is most readable and compact,
but the logic must be instantly understandable.

  toward the SYMBOL WIN field

==================================================

When an X symbol lands during Free Spins:

Required behavior:

5x -> 15x

This must feel satisfying and clearly readable.
