6. MULTIPLIER CORE GRID BEHAVIOR
==================================================

Multiplier Cores exist ONLY during Free Spins.

They can appear during CASCADE REFILLS.

They must physically occupy a grid cell just like another object.

They do NOT count as one of S1-S8.

They NEVER contribute toward the 8+ matching-symbol requirement.

They do not themselves form wins.

They may land during a cascade along with normal symbols.

IMPORTANT:

A Multiplier Core should remain visibly sitting in its cell while the cascade result is evaluated.

If that cascade contains a winning 8+ normal-symbol combination:

all multiplier cores currently present on the board participate.

After the winning-symbol explosion animation:

the Multiplier Cores activate.

They should:

shake slightly
light up
pulse
launch energy toward the win counter

Then apply their value.

After being used:

they disappear before the next cascade/refill.

If the cascade contains no normal-symbol win:

the multiplier cores do NOT create a payout by themselves.

They leave the board when that Free Spin resolves.
